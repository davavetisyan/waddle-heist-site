Waddle Heist

Waddle around the toy shop, grab plush creatures from rival shelves and carry them home before the owner tags you. Collect all 30 for your album.

Support

Found a bug or have a question? Send us a request (a free GitHub account is needed) and include your device model and iOS version. We reply within a few days.

FAQ

Is my progress saved? Your album, stars, fluff, coats and stickers are saved on your device after every round.

Does it work offline? Yes. Ads need an internet connection, but the game itself works offline.

I bought something on another device. Open the Shop on the main menu (a grown-up answers a quick question) and tap Restore.

How do I play? Drag anywhere to waddle. Run into a creature on a rival shelf to grab it, tap your shelf to place it, and tap near a thief to tag them. The highest shelf after 3 minutes wins.
