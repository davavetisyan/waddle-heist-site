Waddle Heist: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Waddle Heist ("the app") is a game made by an independent developer. This policy explains what information the app handles.

Information we collect

Nothing that identifies you. The app has no accounts, no chat and no sign-in, and never asks for your name, email, contacts, photos or location.

Information stored on your device

Your progress (album, stars, fluff, coats, stickers, settings) and your purchases are saved only on your device. Deleting the app removes them; purchases come back with "Restore" in the Shop.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads from Google AdMob: optional bonus videos you choose to watch, and an occasional full-screen ad between rounds (never if you buy Remove Ads). Every ad request is marked as child-directed, so ads are not personalised and are limited to content suitable for everyone. The app does not ask to track you and does not use the Apple Advertising Identifier (IDFA).

To show ads and prevent fraud, AdMob may process:

  • IP address and the approximate (coarse) location derived from it
  • Basic device and app information and ad interactions (which ads were shown and tapped)
  • Diagnostic data related to ads, such as crash and performance information

Google's use of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

In-app purchases

Optional purchases (Remove Ads, Outfit Pack, Album Starter, Season Sticker Book) sit behind a grown-ups-only question and are processed entirely by Apple. We do not receive your payment information.

Children

Waddle Heist is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
